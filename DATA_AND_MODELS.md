## Notes on licensing

- Apache 2.0 (Qwen2.5): permits use, modification, distribution, including commercial.
  Fine-tuned weights (LoRA adapters) can be shared under Apache 2.0.
- CC-BY-SA 4.0 (FLORES-200): permits use with attribution; share-alike applies to
  derivative datasets but not to model weights trained on the data.
- CC-BY-SA 3.0 (Swedish Wikipedia): same share-alike note.
- Synthetic templates: generated entirely by scripts in this repo; no external license
  constraints.
